# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One person organizing their own day: work, groceries, side projects. They open the app on a phone between tasks or keep it beside other windows on a laptop. The job: create a list, add tasks quickly, tick them off and see progress at a glance.

## Product Purpose
A personal to-do app built around "card tasks": each card is a named list with a description, a circular progress indicator and checkable tasks. Success is capturing and completing tasks with as little friction as possible on any screen size.

## Brand Personality
Warm, friendly, light. The orange (#FFB035), Poppins and the rounded, phone-like central panel are the identity and must be kept. Encouraging, never noisy.

## Anti-references
- Dense enterprise task managers (Jira-style tables, toolbars full of options).
- Cold gray SaaS dashboards.
- Overdecorated "fun" UIs with illustrations and constant animation.

## Design Principles
1. Keep the identity: the orange header, round progress and white cards stay; improve them, don't replace them.
2. Progress at a glance: the percentage and completed state must read instantly.
3. Direct manipulation: edit text in place, one tap to check, add or delete.
4. Comfortable on every screen: never cramped on phones, never lost in empty space on large monitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA basics: text contrast at least 4.5:1, body text at least 12-14px, tap targets at least 32px (44px preferred on touch), keyboard-operable controls with visible focus, and respect for prefers-reduced-motion.
